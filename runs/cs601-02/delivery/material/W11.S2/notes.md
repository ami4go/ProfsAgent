## 11. Safety Layers: Guardrails, Monitoring, Fallback

## 12. Reliability Patterns: Circuit Breaker, Retry, Idempotency

- Circuit breaker prevents cascade failures
- Exponential back‑off retry for transient errors
- Idempotent operations enable safe retries
- Pattern composition in pipeline stages

**Notes** (7 min · outcomes CO4.LO2): Explain each pattern with a one‑sentence scenario. Show how they map to requirements: circuit breaker → availability, retry → latency bound, idempotency → data consistency. Mention that AI‑native steps (model inference) often need custom timeouts.

## 17. Summary: Key Takeaways (Summary)
