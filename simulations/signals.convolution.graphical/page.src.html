Graphical convolution · try it yourself

y(t) = ∫ x(τ) h(t − τ) dτ  ·  flip h, slide it to t, multiply by x, and the shaded area is y(t). Change the functions and the values on the right.

t = 0.00   y(t) = 0

Signals

How to write a function

Use t for time. Steps and pulses: u(t) (u(0) = ½), u(t) - u(t - 2), rect(t) (1 for |t| < ½), tri(t), ramp(t). Also exp sin cos abs sqrt log sinc sgn, powers t^2, pi, e. Write 2t or 2*t. W and a are the sliders below.

Example: exp(-a*t)*u(t) or sin(2*pi*t)*(u(t) - u(t - W))

What you see

x(τ)
h(t − τ): h flipped, its origin moved to τ = t
x(τ)·h(t − τ) above 0  below 0
y(t): net shaded area for every t
  1. Flip: h(τ) → h(−τ)
  2. Slide by t: h(t − τ)
  3. Multiply by x(τ)
  4. Net area (green − red) = y(t)
